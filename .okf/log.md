# Update Log

## 2026-10-01
* **Update**: Scoped web access for sub-agents. Arena's attackers, probers and judges (match, recheck, final) may check a deciding factual claim on the web (Parallax via ToolSearch, else WebSearch/WebFetch) and must cite the URL; competitors do not search, and the orchestrator researches changing facts once into the task file instead. Council's developer and stress test search for prior art, failures of similar attempts and cost data; members, the curator and judges stay offline so ideas are not pulled toward the same sources. The default profile now allows the read-only `mcp__parallax` server.
* **Update**: Curated both card decks from a six-agent research pass (EN, ZH, ZH-TW; papers, Hacker News, Reddit, X, linux.do, PTT). `arena/strategies.json` is now 24 reasoning modes x 21 workflows x 20 strategies (10,080 cards), adding Step back, Least to most, Recall related problems, Compute with code, Verify by questions, Independent runs, Guess then refute, Learn from the failed run, Compose your own method, Fix the bottleneck and Label the claims, each with a `source`. `heavy-think/references/perspectives.json` adds a required `delivers` output to every lens (role names alone only change tone), a Bottleneck Hunter lens, 17 techniques (verbalized sampling, ordinary people, TRIZ principles and others), 18 provocations, 6 reframes, 4 decompositions, and `debates` (10 formats) with `debate_rules` (evidence-backed: max 2 rounds, compare against a vote, vary method not persona, 3 or 4 roles when judging). `/debate` is capped at 2 rounds and reads its formats from the deck (`deck.py debates`); council treats judge totals as a sort, not a verdict, since LLM idea rankings are near chance.
* **Update**: Forked the `arena` skill into the hub (`home/.ccp/hub/skills/arena`, MIT, from `Jakeschincariol/arena-skill`) and removed its ccp source, so local changes survive `ccp source update`. Its tests moved with it (`arena/tests`, run with `python3 -m unittest discover -s ~/.claude/skills/arena/tests`). Changes: defenders may borrow up to two declared things from their opponent when they fit their own card; after the final, a learn step (learn, probe, refine, recheck; 5 calls, `--no-learn` to skip) has the champion study the strongest eliminated competitors, keep a LEARN / DON'T LEARN ledger, face two probers, and keep the rethink only if a blind judge prefers it; sub-agents run as a rolling pool of 6 instead of waves of 10; pairing takes the most common reasoning mode first so same-mode matches happen only when forced; `strategies.json` grew to 20 reasoning modes, 16 workflows and 18 strategies (5,760 cards).
* **Update**: The perspective deck is now heavy-think's: `heavy-think/references/perspectives.json` (35 lenses, 18 techniques, 14 provocations, 12 presets each naming a rubric, 10 Unstick reframes and 9 Decompose strategies with situation sets, and rubric weights), dealt by `heavy-think/deck.py`, with criteria anchors in `heavy-think/references/rubric.md` (`creative` and `decision` profiles; `decision` scores running cost). Unstick and Decompose read their strategies from the deck. Brainstorm keeps the obvious answers as a baseline instead of discarding them, deals techniques only for ideation (`--no-technique` for decisions), accepts custom lenses (`Name: question`), and scores with the preset's rubric. The `heavy-thinker` agent follows the same rules.
* **Update**: The `council` skill gained a full mode (`/council full`): a file-based session of 5 to 12 sub-agents driven by `council.py`, modelled on arena's state machine, dealing from heavy-think's deck (council needs heavy-think linked beside it). Members diverge, cross-pollinate over ring rounds (the last round adds the member across the ring), are clustered into a concept pool plus up to 3 orphans kept as written, scored blind by several judges on the chosen rubric profile with a dissent slot, and the shortlist is developed and stress-tested. State lives in `.council/<run>/council.json`.
