## The 3-stage pipeline (apply to every medium)

Every piece of media must pass three sequential biological stages (`Corbetta & Shulman 2002`):

1. **Stage 1 — Sensory Arrest (`0–300 ms`, Ventral Network + V1/A1 + Superior Colliculus):**
   - **Photopic Luminance (`555 nm`) & Color Opponency (`Itti & Koch 2001`; `Camgöz et al. 2004`):** Human photopic vision sums L-cones (`0.2126`) + M-cones (`0.7152`), peaking at `555 nm`. Pure yellow (`#FFD600`, $L = 0.9278$) achieves **19.56:1 contrast on black** (`14.6:1` on `#0F172A`) and fires both intensity and blue-yellow opponent channels.
   - **The Dark Yellow Rule:** Never place yellow text/icons directly on white (`1.07:1` contrast failure). On light backgrounds, put **dark ink (`#0B0E14`) inside a `#FFD600` fill with a dark border (`14.6:1`)**, or switch the accent to Vermilion (`#FF5500`).
   - **Von Restorff Isolation (`1933`) & Motion Onset (`Abrams & Christ 2003`):** Keep `85–90%` of the frame neutral and reserve the peak-contrast accent for **exactly 1 focal target**. Never use infinite looping animations (which habituate); trigger a **single stationary-to-moving Motion Onset (`150–250 ms`, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`)**.
   - **Auditory Mismatch Negativity (`Näätänen 2007`):** In audio/video, cut the background bed and hold **`0.8–1.5 s` of dead silence** before dropping into a lower vocal register (`100–250 ms` pre-attentive MMN wave).

2. **Stage 2 — Epistemic & Dopaminergic Hook (`0.3–15 s`, Dorsal Network + ACC + VTA-Hippocampal Loop):**
   - **Schema-Incongruent Paradox (`Meyer 1997`; `Loewenstein 1994`):** Sensory arrest decays in `1–3 s` (mean short-form swipe decision is `2.8 s`). Collide two familiar beliefs to open a curiosity gap.
   - **Disrupt-Then-Reframe (`Davis & Knowles 1999`) & Numeric Precision (`Janiszewski & Uy 2008`):** Pair a brief cognitive disruption + precise number (`+38.4%`, `1,428 ms`) with an immediate value reframe (`65%` vs `35%` compliance).
   - **The Dopaminergic Curiosity Halo (`Gruber et al. 2014`, *Neuron*):** Place your brand mark, core product name, or key takeaway **inside the open curiosity window** (after posing the paradox, *before* resolving it) to capture **`+20–30%` higher hippocampal memory encoding**.

3. **Stage 3 — Structural Retention & Choice Architecture: Subitizing & Peak–End Encoding (`>15 s`, Prefrontal Cortex):**
   - **Pre-Attentive Subitizing (`<100 ms` for `1–4` items, `Trick & Pylyshyn 1994`) & Cowan's `4 ± 1` Limit (`2001`):** Cap thumbnails to `≤3` elements and rows/navs/tiers to `3–4` chunks (`5+` items force `250–350 ms/item` serial counting).
   - **Reading Perceptual Span (`Rayner 1998`) & Information Scent (`Pirolli & Card 1999`):** Readers extract `3–4` chars left and `14–15` chars right of fixation (`55–68` CPL target; `50–75` acceptable). Front-load the first **`2–3` words (`~11` chars)** of every heading/bullet (`4` CJK chars in `ZH`/`ZH-TW`/`JA`).
   - **Dual-Channel Anti-Redundancy (`Mayer 2009`) & Asymmetric AV Sync (`Vroomen & Keetels 2010`):** Never read on-screen text walls verbatim ($d = 0.72$ penalty). Within one synchronous event, audio must never lead video by `>40 ms` or lag by `>120 ms`; place sound hits `0 to +66 ms` (`0–2` frames) *after* the visual frame. A J-cut is a different case: the next scene's audio leads the cut by `120–150 ms` on purpose.
   - **Endowed Progress (`Nunes & Dreze 2006`) & Peak–End Rule (`Kahneman 1993`):** Start progress bars at `20–25%` (`34%` vs `19%` completion) and cut to black immediately after a final `5–10 s` synthesis peak.

## Gates 1–18

### Stage group I · Sensory Arrest, Spatial Layout, GRIDS & UMSI (Gates 1–6)
- **Gate 1. `555 nm` Luminance & Von Restorff Isolation (`P01–P04`):** `85–90%` of the frame is neutral; only `1` primary CTA/focal target uses `#FFD600` on dark (`>= 14.6:1`) or dark ink `#0B0E14` inside a `#FFD600` fill. Zero yellow text/icons on white (`1.07:1`).
- **Gate 2. Single Motion Onset & Deceleration Physics (`Abrams & Christ 2003`):** Zero infinite blinking/looping animations (which habituate); trigger a single stationary-to-moving onset (`150–300 ms`) with `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.
- **Gate 3. `50 ms` Visceral Gist, Top-Left Entry (`39 px`) & Anti-Banner Blindness (`Still 2018`; `Burke 2005`):** Primary entry anchor sits in the top-left (`80%` left-half gaze); zero critical proof or CTAs in a right-hand sidebar (`0.8%` gaze) or banner-shaped island.
- **Gate 4. Subitizing Limit (`<= 3–4` Objects) & `1px` Bento Chunking (`Cowan 4±1`; `Putkonen 2025, P12`):** Thumbnails/posters have `<= 3` objects (`<100 ms`); each row holds `3–4` cards; high-density Bento cells use `1px` borders with `<= 3` metrics per card and `<= 4` font sizes per screen.
- **Gate 5. Monotonic CIELAB Scales & Dark-is-More Encoding (`Schoenlein 2022, P04`; `Braun & Schloss 2025, P05`):** Data scales map darker/more opaque values to larger quantities (avoiding the `+180–320 ms` inversion penalty) with monotonic lightness (`Delta L* >= 45`); zero rainbow colormaps.
- **Gate 6. `GRIDS` Alignment (`<= 4` Axes) & `UMSI` 3-Peak Heatmap (`Dayama 2020, P11`; `Fosco 2020, P10`):** All elements snap to `<= 4` global alignment groups with `>= 35–45%` whitespace and `<= 3` visual importance peaks in the 3-second UMSI heatmap.

### Stage group II · Empirical A/B Hooks, Reading Psychophysics, DataViz & Multimodal Sync (Gates 7–12)
- **Gate 7. 2-Clause Counterfactual Hook (`H07`) & Deferred Demonstrative (`H01`) (`Upworthy 45,246 A/B Pairs`):** Open with a 2-clause belief violation (`+11.2%` CTR) + forward-referencing demonstrative (`This / Thứ này / Điều này`, `d = +0.100`); zero opening Yes/No questions (`FMEA-01: -12.8%` CTR).
- **Gate 8. Dopamine Curiosity Halo (`+25%`) & Disrupt-Then-Reframe (`35% -> 65%`) (`Gruber 2014`; `Davis & Knowles 1999`):** Place the brand/product/core mechanism *inside* the open curiosity loop before resolving it.
- **Gate 9. `55–68 CPL` Reading Span, `11-Char` Left Scent & Precision Disfluency (`Rayner 1998`; `Alter 2007`):** Keep prose lines at `55–68` characters; front-load the most diagnostic noun/number in the first `2–3` words (`~11` chars); use exact non-round metrics (`$49.87`, `71.47%`) to trigger System 2 evaluation.
- **Gate 10. Cleveland–McGill Aligned Bars (`1.0x` Error), Martini Glass & Zero-Bullet Slides (`Cleveland 1984`; `Segel & Heer 2010, P22`; `Mayer 2009`):** Use aligned bar/dot charts (`1.0x` error) with `1` `#FFD600` callout bar instead of pie charts (`1.95–2.6x` error); slide decks use `<= 9`-word assertion headlines and zero multi-line bullets (avoiding `-28%` redundancy loss).
- **Gate 11. Zero `0–3s` Greeting Bounce & `9:16` Vertical / Danmaku Safe Zone (`Baughan 2021, P08`):** Video/podcast starts in motion or paradox at `0.0s` (avoiding `+37%` bounce); keep faces and captions inside `Y = 25%–72%, X = 10%–85%`.
- **Gate 12. `0.8–1.5s` Acoustic MMN Silence, `160–185 WPM` & Asymmetric AV Sync (`Näätänen 2007`; `Rodero 2016`; `Vroomen 2010, P33`):** Drop background bed for `0.8–1.5s` before core thesis statements; maintain `160–185 WPM` (slowing cuts during complex claims per LC4MP); on a J-cut the next scene's audio leads the visual cut by `120–150 ms` (pre-orienting); within one synchronous event audio never leads video by `>40 ms` or lags by `>120 ms` (`Vatakis & Spence 2007`); impact SFX land at `0..+40 ms`.

### Stage group III · Neural Rhythm, 5-Quintile Storyboard, Dual-Mode CIELAB & 5-Vector Ruptures (Gates 13–18)
- **Gate 13. `1.5–2 Hz` Neural Entrainment & `E -> I -> P -> R` Storyboard Grammar (`Lakatos 2019, P25`; `Cohn 2013, P17`):** Alternate `60%` medium clauses (`12–20` words) with `25%` short punch clauses (`<7` words, `r = +0.104` interest); sequence panels/scenes as `Establisher -> Initial -> Prolongation -> Peak -> Release` with zero `Paraphrase` filler before the Peak.
- **Gate 14. 5-Quintile Narrative Arc (`Q1–Q5`), `Q1` Human Grounding & Zero `NL1–NL4` Loopholes (`Boyd 2020, P19`; `Reagan 2016, P18`):** Open `Q1` (`0–20%`) with Concrete Human Grounding (`r = +0.149`) + Episodic Story Hook (`z = +4.40`), eliminating abstract academic sludge (`NL2: +19.6%` boredom) and ungrounded rhetorical question barrages (`NL1: +29.8%` confusion); peak Cognitive Tension in `Q2–Q3`.
- **Gate 15. Single Primary Attention Flavor (`F1–F6`) & Curved/Sharp Morphology (`Silvia 2008, P23`; `Bar & Neta 2006, P26`):** Target one primary attention flavor (`F1` Epistemic Curiosity, `F2` High-Arousal Awe, `F3` Indignation, `F4` Elevation, `F5` Social Warmth & Levity, `F6` Boredom, Overload & Backfire, which is a failure state to avoid, never a target); use `12–24px` curved containers for approach/safety (`67.2%` vs `37.9%` preference) and reserve `0–2px` sharp angles (`<= 15%` area) for conflict/alerts.
- **Gate 16. Dual-Mode CIELAB Color Physics: Ambient Carrier vs. Orthogonal Peak/CTA Impulse (`Schloss & Palmer 2011, P27`; `Ou et al. 2004/2010, P28/P32`):** Keep `92–95%` background area on a smooth Continuous Affective Carrier (`Delta H* <= 45 deg`, `Delta L* <= 15` across `Q1->Q5`) and fire an Orthogonal Chromatic Impulse (`Delta E*ab >= 45`, `Delta L* >= 40`) on `<= 5–8%` area at Peak `P*` and the primary CTA (zero `BC1–BC3` failures).
- **Gate 17. 5-Vector Causal Pattern Ruptures (`V1–V5`) & Zero Seductive Details (`Harp & Mayer 1998, P31`; `Zacks 2007, P20`):** Every `15–25s` (video) or `2` scroll viewports (web), fire a thesis-coupled rupture (`V1` Subtraction, `V2` Abstraction Jump, `V3` Cross-Modal Pre-Echo, `V4` Topology Rupture, `V5` Agency Flip); eliminate 100% of tangential B-roll/seductive details (`-55%` recall, `-62%` transfer).
- **Gate 18. Conditional Peak–End Resolution (`Improver 81.8%`) & `20%` Endowed Progress (`Kahneman et al. 1993, P34`; `Nunes & Dreze 2006`):** Start progress bars at `20–25%` endowed progress; ensure final quintile `Q5` delivers emotional relief/resolution (`Q5 > Q3`, `81.8%` repeat preference vs `45.5%` for non-improvers) and closes with a single concrete action.

### Gates 23–26: metric and pass threshold

1. **Gate 23 — The Sensorimotor Concreteness & Active-Listening Gate (`P51–P54`):**
   - **Metric:** Mean Brysbaert lexical concreteness score $C \in [1.0, 5.0]$ of content nouns/verbs in Hero, Hook, and Support Replies + count of bare abstract pro-forms (*this, that, issue, item, solution, optimize*).
   - **Pass Threshold:** Hero/Hook/CTA content words achieve **$C \ge 3.5$**, include **$\ge 1$ Level-1/2 Concrete Action Verb (`DAV/IAV`)**, and contain **`0` unanchored pro-forms** (*"this issue"*, *"that item"*).
2. **Gate 24 — The Click-vs.-Share Affective Polarity Gate (`P14`, `P55`):**
   - **Metric:** Presence of threat/fault arrest words in the cold headline/hook vs. high-arousal activating emotion (**Awe / Anger / Practical Utility / Interest**) in the body/payoff.
   - **Pass Threshold:** Cold hooks contain **1–2 concrete threat/cost/paradox words** (`+2.3%` CTR/word), while the payoff delivers **high-arousal Awe (`+30%`) or Practical Utility (`+30%`)** with **zero low-arousal sadness/helplessness (`-16%`)** at the close.
3. **Gate 25 — The Pronoun, Noun-Identity & Rhetorical-Involvement Gate (`P56–P60, P64`):**
   - **Metric:** Pronoun distribution (`"I"` vs. `"We"` vs. `"You"` in errors), verb-vs.-noun CTA framing, and rhetorical question placement relative to audience involvement.
   - **Pass Threshold:** (a) Specialist/support/founder copy uses **`"I / Mình"`** rather than institutional `"We"`, and error states contain **`0` accusatory `"You"` clauses** (`P56`); (b) Rhetorical questions appear **only** on low-involvement top-of-funnel hooks with strong proof and are **0%** on high-involvement pricing/checkout/docs pages (`P64`).
4. **Gate 26 — The Syntactic Locality, PAD Threshold & Mental Accounting Gate (`P61–P63, P65–P74`):**
   - **Metric:** Intervening discourse referents $n$ between Subject and Main Verb (`P63`), daily price framing amount (`P67`), fee integration (`P65`), and Spotlight/IOED scaffolding (`P71, P74`).
   - **Pass Threshold:** (a) Subject-to-Verb distance has **$n \le 1$ intervening discourse referent** (`≤ 5 words`) with **0 center-embedded clauses** (`P63`) and preserves *that/because/rằng* before high-surprisal clauses (`P62`); (b) Pennies-a-Day framing is used **only when $\le \$2.50/\text{day}$ (`~50k VND/ngày`)** (`P67`); (c) Checkout fees are **integrated** into a single price while bonuses/`$0 FREE` perks are **segregated** (`P65, P68`); (d) Public/collaborative onboarding includes an explicit **Private-by-Default Spotlight Shield** (`P74`).
