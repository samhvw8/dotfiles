# Systematic reviews: AMSTAR 2 and ROBIS

A review is only as good as its search, its appraisal of included studies and its
synthesis. Never take a review's conclusion without appraising the review.

| Tool | Use for | Output |
|------|---------|--------|
| AMSTAR 2 (BMJ 2017;358:j4008) | Reviews of healthcare interventions (RCTs and/or NRSI) | Overall confidence: High, Moderate, Low, Critically low |
| ROBIS (version 1.2) | Reviews of interventions, diagnosis, prognosis or aetiology | Risk of bias in the review: Low, High, Unclear |

Use AMSTAR 2 for intervention reviews; add or switch to ROBIS for non-intervention
reviews or when a guideline asks for risk of bias.

## AMSTAR 2: 16 items (critical items in bold)

1. PICO components in the question and inclusion criteria
2. **Protocol registered before the review began; deviations justified**
3. Choice of study designs explained
4. **Comprehensive literature search** (≥2 databases, keywords and strategy, grey literature, registries, recent)
5. Study selection in duplicate
6. Data extraction in duplicate
7. **List of excluded studies with reasons**
8. Included studies described in enough detail
9. **Satisfactory technique for assessing risk of bias in individual studies** (RCTs and NRSI separately)
10. Funding sources of included studies reported
11. **Appropriate meta-analysis methods** (if pooled)
12. Impact of risk of bias on the pooled results assessed (if pooled)
13. **Risk of bias accounted for when interpreting results**
14. Heterogeneity explained and discussed
15. **Publication bias investigated and its impact discussed** (if pooled)
16. Conflicts of interest of the review, including funding, reported

Answers: Yes, Partial yes, No. Do not sum to a score.

| Overall confidence | Rule |
|--------------------|------|
| High | No or one non-critical weakness |
| Moderate | More than one non-critical weakness, no critical flaw |
| Low | One critical flaw, with or without non-critical weaknesses |
| Critically low | More than one critical flaw; do not rely on the review |

Many non-critical weaknesses can move Moderate down to Low. Appraisers may add a
critical item (for example item 3 for a harms review that excluded NRSI).

## ROBIS: three phases

1. Assess relevance (optional): does the review's PICO match your question?
2. Identify concerns in four domains:
   - D1 Study eligibility criteria (pre-specified, appropriate, unambiguous?)
   - D2 Identification and selection of studies (search breadth, duplicate screening)
   - D3 Data collection and study appraisal (duplicate extraction, risk-of-bias tool used)
   - D4 Synthesis and findings (all studies included, heterogeneity, robustness, bias addressed)
   Each domain: low, high or unclear concern.
3. Judge overall risk of bias in the review: did the authors' conclusions account
   for the concerns found?

## Where to look

`section: "Methods"` (protocol/PROSPERO, search, selection, risk of bias),
supplementary search strategy, `section: "Results"` (PRISMA flow, forest plots),
`query: "excluded studies"`, `query: "funnel plot"`.

## Feeds GRADE as

A review's own GRADE table is a claim. Check it against your AMSTAR 2 / ROBIS
appraisal; a Critically low review's certainty ratings are not reused.
