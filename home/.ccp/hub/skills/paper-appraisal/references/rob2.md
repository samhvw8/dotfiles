# RoB 2: randomised trials

Version: RoB 2, 22 August 2019 (BMJ 2019;366:l4898). Variants for cluster-randomised
trials (revised 18 Mar 2021) and crossover trials (revised 18 Mar 2021). Assess one
**result** (outcome + analysis), for one effect of interest:
- **Assignment** (intention-to-treat effect), or
- **Adhering** (per-protocol effect). Say which.

Answers: Yes, Probably yes, Probably no, No, No information.
Domain and overall levels: **Low risk**, **Some concerns**, **High risk**.

## Domains and signalling questions (short form)

**D1. Bias arising from the randomisation process**
- 1.1 Was the allocation sequence random?
- 1.2 Was it concealed until participants were enrolled and assigned?
- 1.3 Do baseline differences suggest a problem with randomisation?

**D2. Bias due to deviations from intended interventions**
Assignment effect:
- 2.1/2.2 Were participants / carers and deliverers aware of assignment?
- 2.3 If so, were there deviations that arose because of the trial context?
- 2.4/2.5 Were they likely to affect the outcome? Balanced between groups?
- 2.6 Was the analysis appropriate (ITT or modified ITT)?
- 2.7 If not, could analysing people outside their randomised group change the result substantially?
Adhering effect: balanced non-protocol interventions? implementation failures?
non-adherence? an analysis able to estimate the effect of adhering?

**D3. Bias due to missing outcome data**
- 3.1 Were data available for all, or nearly all, participants?
- 3.2 Is there evidence the result is not biased by missing data (sensitivity analyses)?
- 3.3 Could missingness depend on the true value of the outcome?
- 3.4 Is it likely that it did?

**D4. Bias in measurement of the outcome**
- 4.1 Was the measurement method inappropriate?
- 4.2 Could measurement differ between groups?
- 4.3 Were assessors aware of the intervention received?
- 4.4 Could knowledge of it have influenced the assessment?
- 4.5 Is it likely that it did?

**D5. Bias in selection of the reported result**
- 5.1 Was the analysis done to a pre-specified plan, finalised before unblinded data were available?
- 5.2 Was the result selected from multiple outcome measurements (scales, definitions, time points)?
- 5.3 Was it selected from multiple analyses of the data?
Compare against the registry entry and the protocol / SAP.

## Overall judgement

| Overall | When |
|---------|------|
| Low | Low in all domains |
| Some concerns | Some concerns in at least one domain, none high |
| High | High in at least one domain, or some concerns in several domains that together lower confidence substantially |

## Where to look in the full text

`section: "Methods"` (randomisation, allocation concealment, blinding), the CONSORT
flow diagram (`query: "lost to follow-up"`), `section: "Statistical analysis"`,
the registry ID (Open science items 2-4 in CONSORT 2025).

## Feeds GRADE as

Risk of bias for the outcome: weight of high-risk and some-concerns trials in the
pooled result.
