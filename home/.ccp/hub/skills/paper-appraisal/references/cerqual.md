# GRADE-CERQual: qualitative evidence syntheses

Use for findings of a qualitative evidence synthesis (themes, experiences,
barriers). GRADE certainty does not apply to these findings.

Confidence is assessed **per review finding**, from four components.

| Component | Ask |
|-----------|-----|
| Methodological limitations | Concerns about the design or conduct of the primary studies behind the finding (appraise each with a qualitative tool, e.g. CASP or the JBI qualitative checklist) |
| Coherence | Is the finding well grounded in the primary data? Do contradicting or ambiguous data exist? |
| Adequacy of data | Richness and quantity of the data behind the finding |
| Relevance | How well the contexts of the primary studies match the review question (population, setting, phenomenon) |

A fifth component, dissemination bias, is under development; mention it only as
a possible concern.

## Levels

High, Moderate, Low, Very low confidence that the finding is a reasonable
representation of the phenomenon. Start from High and lower for serious concerns
in any component.

## Output per finding

Finding, contributing studies, concern per component (no or very minor / minor /
moderate / serious) with a reason, overall confidence, explanation.

## Where to look

`section: "Methods"` (sampling, data collection, analysis approach, reflexivity),
`section: "Results"` with quoted participant data supporting each theme.
