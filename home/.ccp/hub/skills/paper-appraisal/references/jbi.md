# JBI critical appraisal checklists: other designs

Use JBI for designs the Cochrane-family tools do not cover. JBI has revised its
suite (RCT tool 2023, quasi-experimental tool 2024) to align with risk-of-bias
methods; download the current checklist from jbi.global/critical-appraisal-tools
and name its year.

| Design | JBI checklist |
|--------|---------------|
| Case series | Checklist for Case Series |
| Case report | Checklist for Case Reports |
| Analytical cross-sectional | Assessment of Risk of Bias for Analytical Cross-sectional Studies |
| Prevalence | Checklist for Prevalence Studies |
| Case-control | Checklist for Case Control Studies |
| Cohort (when ROBINS-I/E does not fit) | Checklist for Cohort Studies |
| Quasi-experimental | Checklist for Quasi-Experimental Studies |
| Economic evaluation | Checklist for Economic Evaluations |
| Qualitative research | Checklist for Qualitative Research |
| Expert opinion, narrative, policy | Checklists for Textual Evidence |

Prefer RoB 2 for RCTs, ROBINS-I V2 / ROBINS-E for non-randomised studies, QUADAS-3
for test accuracy. Use JBI's RCT or cohort checklists only when a review protocol
asks for them.

## How to apply

- Answer each question Yes, No, Unclear, or Not applicable, with a quoted sentence.
- Do not sum "yes" answers into a score. Say which "no" answers matter for the
  question and why.
- Case series and case reports cannot show an effect. They show that something
  can happen. Say this when they are the only evidence.

## Common questions across JBI checklists (short form)

- Clear inclusion criteria and consecutive / complete inclusion (case series)?
- Condition and exposure measured validly and the same way for all?
- Confounders identified and handled (analytical designs)?
- Outcomes measured validly; follow-up complete and long enough?
- Appropriate statistical analysis; demographics and site reported?

## Feeds GRADE as

Usually NRSI starting at Low (conventional GRADE). Case series and reports seldom
move above Very low.
